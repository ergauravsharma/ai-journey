   CloudDesk Refund Policy

   Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of their initial purchase. After 30 days, refunds are not available, but you may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

   To request a refund, contact support through the billing page in your account settings. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days and returned to the original payment method.

   If you were charged twice for the same subscription period, the duplicate charge is refunded automatically once our billing team confirms it. Annual plans are refundable within 14 days of purchase, not 30. Enterprise contracts follow the terms written in the signed agreement, not this policy.
